A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before
you go.

Need a hand? Call us ↗

How do I book a ride?+

Choose your pickup and destination in Alpha Ride, review the ride option and quoted fare, then request your ride. You can also call 8888 to book with the team.

Can I book without a smartphone?+

Yes. Call 8888 and tell the team your pickup location and destination.

How do I contact my driver?+

After a driver accepts your request, their profile and available contact details appear on your active ride screen.

How can I join as a driver?+

Use Alpha Plus to register, add your vehicle, and submit the required documents. Your registration must be approved before you can go online and receive rides.
